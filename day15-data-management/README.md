# Day 15 - Data Management

## Data Quality Problems

Data quality is very important in Salesforce and CRM systems. Poor-quality data can create many business problems.

### Common Data Quality Problems

* Missing customer information
* Incorrect phone numbers or emails
* Duplicate records
* Outdated data
* Inconsistent formatting
* Invalid entries

### Effects of Poor Data Quality

* Wrong business decisions
* Reduced productivity
* Poor customer experience
* Reporting inaccuracies

Maintaining high-quality data improves business efficiency and reliability.

---

## Migration Discussion

Data migration is the process of moving data from one system to another.

### Migration Activities

* Extracting existing data
* Cleaning and validating data
* Transforming data formats
* Importing data into Salesforce
* Verifying migration success

### Challenges During Migration

* Data loss risks
* Duplicate records
* Incorrect mappings
* Compatibility issues
* Large data volume handling

Proper planning is important for successful data migration.

---

## Duplicate Prevention Ideas

Duplicate records create confusion and reduce system accuracy.

### Methods to Prevent Duplicates

* Validation rules
* Duplicate matching rules
* Required unique fields
* Automated duplicate checks
* Standardized data entry formats

### Benefits of Duplicate Prevention

* Cleaner database
* Accurate reporting
* Better customer management
* Improved user productivity

Duplicate prevention improves overall CRM efficiency.

---

## Enterprise Risks of Bad Data

Bad data can create serious risks for enterprise organizations.

### Enterprise Risks

* Financial losses
* Incorrect business reports
* Customer dissatisfaction
* Security and compliance issues
* Operational inefficiencies
* Poor decision-making

### Risk Reduction Methods

* Regular data audits
* User training
* Data validation processes
* Backup and recovery strategies
* Governance policies

Organizations must manage data carefully to avoid business risks.
